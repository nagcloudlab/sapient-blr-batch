# ServiceNow -- Project Brief
## Module 36 | Days 43-44

---

## Sustain Context

FoodExpress has deployed ServiceNow as its ITSM platform, but the initial configuration has several issues: SLA policies with wrong triggers, email notifications with broken templates, and an instance with security gaps. As a sustain engineer, you must fix these configurations and demonstrate competence in ITSM processes through an MCQ assessment and a role play exercise.

---
