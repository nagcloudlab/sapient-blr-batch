LAB 07 — CMDBWHY THIS MATTERS
Without CMDB, You're Flying Blind

UPI processes 10 billion+ transactions/month across 350+ banks.

When the Transaction Service goes down at 2 AM, the NOC team needs to instantly know:

  • What depends on this service?
  • What infrastructure does it run on?
  • Who owns it and who should be paged?
  • What SLAs are at risk?
  • What's the blast radius of this outage?
CMDBThe single source of truth for every component, relationship, and dependency in your infrastructure
Without CMDB: "Which server is this service on?" → 30 min of Slack threads. With CMDB: one click on the Service Map.

ITIL 4 DEFINITION
"Ensure accurate and reliable information about the configuration of services, and the CIs that support them, is available when and where it is needed."

The CI Lifecycle (4 Stages)

StageQuestionUPI Example

WHY INHERITANCE MATTERS
Each child table adds specialized fields. cmdb_ci_db_instance has port, DB type, version. cmdb_ci_server has RAM, CPU, OS. Choose the right class = get the right fields.

RelationshipMeaningUPI Example
Depends on :: Used byService A needs Service B to functionTransaction Service depends on PostgreSQL Primary
Runs on :: RunsApplication hosted on serverTransaction Service runs on App Server 01
Contains :: Contained byParent groups childrenUPI Payment Platform contains all 11 CIs
Monitors :: Monitored byMonitoring tool watches a CIPrometheus monitors Transaction Service

Step 3: View the Service Map

Open UPI Payment Platform CI → click View Map to see the auto-generated dependency visualization.

What the Service Map Shows

  • Upstream impact: What depends on this CI? (LB → Txn Svc → PostgreSQL)
  • Downstream dependencies: What does this CI need? (Txn Svc needs PostgreSQL + App Server)
  • Blast radius: If this CI fails, how many services are affected?
  • Infrastructure view: Servers, databases, load balancers in one diagram

  • Change Operational Status to In Maintenance
  • Check the History tab to see the audit trail
  • Change it back to Operational
In production, CI status changes should go through Change Management (Lab 15). Never change a CI's status without a change record.

LAB 07 — CMDBGOVERNANCE
CMDB Health & Best Practices
CheckWhat to Look ForHow to Fix
Orphan CIsCIs with zero relationshipsAdd dependencies or mark as retired
Duplicate CIsSame server registered twiceMerge records, enable dedup rules
Stale CIsLast updated > 90 days agoVerify with infrastructure team, retire if decomm'd
Missing OwnersNo support group assignedEvery CI must have a support group

CMDB Governance Rules

  • Every CI must have: Name, Class, Operational Status, Support Group, Owner
  • Every CI must have at least 1 relationship
  • Review CMDB Health Dashboard monthly
  • CI changes require a Change Request (Lab 15)
