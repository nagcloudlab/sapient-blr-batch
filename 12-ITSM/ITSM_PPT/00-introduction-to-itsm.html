Teaching with the animated story

Press Right / Space to reveal the next idea, table row or animation step. The scene and dialogue give you a moment to ask what is happening.

R replays the current slide. A shows all. Shift + Right / Left skips slides.

S starts or pauses automatic progression. Choose 5, 8 or 12 seconds using Pace. Playback pauses at discussion questions so you can collect responses. The stories are silent.

N opens slide notes. P opens the separate trainer window. Share only the slide window with learners. O opens the slide list, G opens terms, M opens the Markdown map and F enters full screen.

All illustrations, fonts and code are included for offline use. Keep the assets folder beside the HTML files. Reduced-motion preferences are respected. Browser file previews may require “Open in browser”.
