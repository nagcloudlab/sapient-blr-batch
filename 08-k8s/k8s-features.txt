how to define resource in k8s? 

    - using YAML or JSON manifest files

    ------------------------
    |apiVersion: v1
    |kind: Pod
    |
    |metadata:
    |  name: my-pod
    |  labels:
    |    app: my-app
    |
    |spec:
    |  containers:
    |  - name: my-container
    |    image: my-image
    ------------------------
