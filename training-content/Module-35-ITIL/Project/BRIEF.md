# ITIL Practices -- Project Brief
## Module 35 | Days 41-42

---

## Sustain Context

FoodExpress has adopted ITIL practices but the documentation is inconsistent and contains errors. As a sustain engineer, you must fix the ITIL process templates to ensure incidents are handled correctly, changes are managed safely, problems are properly analyzed, and SLAs are realistic and enforceable.

---

## Task Table

| # | Task | Description | Duration | Points |
|---|------|-------------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Fix Incident Record | Fix 7 bugs: priority, category, SLA, assignment, status flow, CI, communication | 30 min | 12 |
| 2 | Fix Change Request | Fix 6 bugs: change type, risk, implementation plan, rollback, window, CAB | 25 min | 10 |
| 3 | Fix Problem Record | Fix 6 bugs: status, incidents, 5 Whys, workaround, permanent fix, trend | 25 min | 10 |
| 4 | Fix SLA Definition | Fix 6 bugs: availability target, resolution time, measurement, penalties, exclusions, reporting | 25 min | 8 |
| 5 | Practice Mapping | Map 8 FoodExpress scenarios to correct ITIL practices | 20 min | 5 |
| 6 | Bonus: Integration | Trace one FoodExpress issue through Incident > Problem > Change > Release | 15 min | 5 |

**Total Points Available:** 50
