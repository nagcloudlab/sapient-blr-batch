# Evidence Templates

Use these templates to document your work throughout the training.

## Contents

- `fix-report.md` -- Template for documenting bug fixes
- `screenshot-guide.md` -- Screenshot naming convention and guidelines
- `demo-outline.md` -- Presentation outline for capsule project demos

---

## When to Use

- **fix-report.md:** After every lab bug fix. Fill in one section per bug.
- **screenshot-guide.md:** When capturing evidence of your fixes working.
- **demo-outline.md:** During capsule days (M08, M11, M17, M23, M31, M37) for team presentations.
