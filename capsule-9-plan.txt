
  ---
  What Students Should Add to Their Capstone

  1. ITSM Process Documents (ITIL 4 aligned)
  - Incident Management workflow for their domain (P1-P4 classification, escalation matrix)
  - Problem Management — RCA template with at least 1 known error record
  - Change Management — RFC template, change types (standard/normal/emergency), CAB approval flow
  - Service Catalog — 3-5 service offerings for their domain with SLA targets

  2. ServiceNow Configuration (screenshots or export)
  - At least 2 custom tables relevant to their domain
  - User/Group/Role setup with role tagging
  - SLA definitions with triggers and schedules
  - 1 catalog item with variables
  - Change request workflow (standard + normal)
  - Email notification rule

  3. Integration with Prior Work
  - Map SRE SLOs (from capsule-8) to ITIL SLA definitions
  - Link Prometheus/Grafana alerts → incident creation workflow
  - Show how observability feeds into problem management (recurring alerts → problem record → known error)

  ---
  Submission Format

  Each student submits a folder/zip containing:
  capstone-project/
  ├── itsm/
  │   ├── incident-management.md      # workflow, priority matrix, escalation
  │   ├── problem-management.md       # RCA template, known error DB
  │   ├── change-management.md        # RFC template, change types, CAB
  │   └── service-catalog.md          # service offerings + SLA targets
  ├── servicenow/
  │   ├── screenshots/                # table config, SLA, catalog, notifications
  │   └── configuration-notes.md      # what was configured and why
  └── integration-map.md              # SRE → ITSM mapping

  ---
  Demo Flow (5-7 min per student)

  1. Service Catalog (1 min) — Show domain services and SLA targets
  2. Incident → Problem flow (2 min) — Walk through a realistic incident, show how it escalates, links to problem
  record
  3. ServiceNow walkthrough (2 min) — Show configured tables, SLA, catalog item, change request
  4. SRE-to-ITSM bridge (1-2 min) — How monitoring alerts map to incident tickets, how error budgets inform change
  freeze decisions
